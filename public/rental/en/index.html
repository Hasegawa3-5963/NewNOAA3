JOYCART RENTAL

Go farther, without worrying about the distance.

Rent a compact, foldable electric mobility cart for travel in Japan or for a trial before purchase.

Your JOYcart is delivered in a dedicated travel case to your home or hotel. It can be folded and carried by car, train, Shinkansen, airplane, or cruise ship, subject to each operator's requirements.

HOW IT WORKS

Four simple steps

Book

Select the rental period and delivery/return method, then pay online by credit card.

Receive

The cart is delivered to your home or hotel front desk in its dedicated case.

Travel

Use it around your hotel, at attractions, and in accessible facilities.

Return

Return shipping is available nationwide, including from a location different from delivery.

Please contact us in advance if your accommodation does not have a front desk. Airline, railway, bus, and cruise policies vary; confirm requirements with each operator before travel.
